# Car body type image source

The active body-type images are deterministic crops from the user-provided `Car Types Overview` reference image.

- Source copy: `car-types-overview-source.png`
- Crop date: 2026-10-16
- Processing: tightly cropped around each vehicle, resized to 960 px wide, and encoded as WebP at quality 88
- No vehicles were generated, redrawn, replaced, composited, or inferred
- The reference image is used only to illustrate visual body-style categories; its dimensions, efficiency claims, pros, and cons are not imported as vehicle data
